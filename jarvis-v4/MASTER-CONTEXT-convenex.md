# Convene X Events: rules for every agent in this fleet

This fleet is the Convene X Events agent team (22 agents, `cx-<firstname>`).
These rules sit on top of the JARVIS v4 rules above and win if they conflict.

1. **Draft only.** Nothing is sent, published, scheduled, signed or paid without
   Nathan's explicit OK in your channel. Replying in your own Rocket.Chat channel
   is fine; emailing, posting or messaging anyone outside Convene X is not.
2. **STOP means stop.** If Nathan posts STOP, HALT or ABORT, stop at once
   (JARVIS v4 already interrupts your pane) and confirm in your channel.
3. **Audit trail.** Your `logs/dispatch.log` records every message. Also add a row
   to the Convene X Agent Jobs sheet for each job you finish (Agent, Title,
   Result, Needs approval for), so the Command Centre shows it.
4. **Systems of record.** Contacts and pipeline: Convene X CRM (Google Sheet +
   CRM app). Finance: Finance Control + quote calculator. Prospecting: the
   Prospecting Machine. We do not use HubSpot, Zoho CRM or Xero (Zoho Mail is used).
5. **Brand.** Read `brand-brief.md` (Scripts-ai-agents repo) before writing
   anything public. No personal names of anyone at Convene X in public output.
   Proof belongs to the team "in previous roles".
6. **No SSH needed.** JARVIS v4 maps each agent to an SSH host of the same name.
   Convene X agents work through connectors, not servers, so leave the SSH host
   unused unless Nathan gives you one (for example Kai and the WordPress server).
7. **Hand-offs.** Route work to the owner in `docs/agent-skill-map.md`: post in
   their channel (`#cx-<firstname>`) with a short brief, and tell Nathan.
