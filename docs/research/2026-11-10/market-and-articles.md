# Market and article research: small executive events (10 Nov 2026)

**For:** Leo (week-long social campaign), via Freya. **Prepared by:** Competitive Intelligence.
**Topics:** small corporate events, executive dinners, virtual roundtables, B2B webinars. Audience: senior B2B buyers, UK and US.

**Scope and method.** Public web research only (Firecrawl search and scrape), 10 Nov 2026. No licensed SEO or traffic data is connected (no Semrush, Ahrefs or DataForSEO), so nothing here states a competitor's traffic or ranking as fact. "Ranks" means it appeared in a Firecrawl web search on 10 Nov 2026, one snapshot, not a verified Google position. No CRM lost-deal check: Convene X hasn't run its first event yet, so there's no first-party signal to check. No outreach, no contact with anyone.

**Labels used:**
- **Observed:** read on the page.
- **Inferred:** my reading of the pattern.
- **Vendor data:** published by a company that sells event tech or services. It's real, but it's marketing. Use it with the source named.
- **UNVERIFIED:** couldn't trace it to a primary source. Don't use it in public copy.

## 1. Market and trend picture (Nov 2025 to Nov 2026, weighted to Aug to Nov 2026)

### What's changing

| Shift | Evidence (observed) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Fewer, smaller, better events** | Nearly 70% of event leaders are cutting the number of events they run but protecting the attendee experience. Most plan more intimate networking and sub-200-person events, and only 18% plan more large hosted events. ([Forrester, Q1 2026 State of B2B Events, 400+ decision-makers, blog 25 Jun 2026](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/the-next-era-of-b2b-events-eight-data-backed-shifts-defining-2026/)) | Strong. Analyst firm, matches Amex GBT and Bizzabo |
| **Participation over presentation** | 2 in 3 organisations plan more workshops, roundtables and structured networking. Quote from the survey: "If you go to an in-person event and feel like you're watching TV, then what's the point?" ([Forrester, Jun 2026](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/the-next-era-of-b2b-events-eight-data-backed-shifts-defining-2026/)) | Strong |
| **Attendee experience is the top priority going into 2027** | It's the top priority in every region. Attendees expect more personalisation (35%), more social activity and networking (31%) and more hands-on, interactive sessions (31%). 91% expect meetings spend to rise in 2027. Fieldwork ran 5 to 14 Aug 2026. ([Amex GBT 2027 Global Meetings and Events Forecast, press release 13 Oct 2026](https://www.amexglobalbusinesstravel.com/press-releases/2027-global-meetings-events-forecast/)) | Strong. **Last 90 days** |
| **Networking is losing credibility** | Only 15% of organisers rate their networking as "very effective", down from 46% in 2025. ([Bizzabo 2026 State of Events Benchmark](https://www.bizzabo.com/blog/event-industry-trends-2026), [report page](https://welcome.bizzabo.com/state-of-events-2026)) | Moderate. Vendor survey, but a big drop |
| **ROI scrutiny and a leadership perception gap** | About half of organisations say their events perform well, but only 38% of CMOs agree. For more than half, net-new lead gen is the primary objective, which Forrester warns "risks undervaluing" relationship value. ([Forrester, Jun 2026](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/the-next-era-of-b2b-events-eight-data-backed-shifts-defining-2026/)) | Strong |
| **Attribution is the sore spot** | 86% can't accurately attribute ROI to events. 90% say events influence deals that never get CRM credit. 44% call events "influential for pipeline but hard to measure". (Vendor data: [Vendelux 2026 B2B Events Survey "Events Are in the Hot Seat", 120+ respondents, PDF](https://6332430.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/6332430/2026%20B2B%20Events%20Report/Events%20Are%20In%20The%20Hot%20Seat.pdf)) Separately, 40% of organisers report difficulty proving ROI, down from 70% in 2025 ([Bizzabo 2026](https://www.bizzabo.com/blog/how-to-prove-sponsor-roi-events)) | Moderate. Small vendor sample |
| **Sponsors want evidence, not logos** | "The visibility-first model is broken": sponsors expect data on attendance, engagement and lead quality, and pipeline influenced. ([Bizzabo, How to Prove Sponsor ROI, 2026](https://www.bizzabo.com/blog/how-to-prove-sponsor-roi-events)) Two-thirds have now integrated their event platform with the sales and marketing stack, up 44% year on year ([Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/the-next-era-of-b2b-events-eight-data-backed-shifts-defining-2026/)) | Moderate |
| **Virtual is back, but small** | The number of organisations planning more virtual events doubled year on year ([Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/the-next-era-of-b2b-events-eight-data-backed-shifts-defining-2026/)). Webinars with fewer than 100 registrants get 6% higher live attendance (vendor data, [Contrast 2026](https://www.getcontrast.io/learn/webinar-statistics)). 71% of webinars have fewer than 100 live attendees (vendor data, [Univid 2026](https://univid.io/webinar-statistics/)) | Moderate |
| **Webinar attendance ranges 40 to 50% live, depending on the platform** | Goldcast: 40% average across 26,190 webinars in 2025 ([report](https://www.goldcast.io/reports/b2b-webinar-benchmark-report-2026)). Livestorm: 47.7% across 33,786 sessions ([2026](https://livestorm.co/blog/boost-webinar-attendance-rate)). ON24's 60% counts on-demand viewers. Definitions differ, so don't compare them directly ([AInora's audit of the studies, 2026](https://ainora.lt/blog/webinar-attendance-statistics-every-study-2026)) | Moderate. All vendor data |
| **Buyers avoid reps, but still want humans to check AI** | 67% of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free experience (n≈650; [Gartner, 9 Mar 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-09-gartner-sales-survey-finds-67-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-experience)), up from 61% in 2025. 69% turn to sales reps to validate AI-generated insights ([Gartner, 20 May 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-20-gartner-survey-finds-sixty-nine-percent-of-b-two-b-buyers-turn-to-sales-reps-to-validate-ai-generated-insights)) | Strong. **Inferred implication:** buyers want expert and peer input without being sold to, which is exactly the space a no-pitch table fills |
| **AI and outreach fatigue makes in-person interaction worth more** | Widely claimed. The figures circulating (82% email fatigue, 94% use LLMs, 77% trust more after in-person) trace to secondary aggregation ([Ei Advisory report](https://ei-a.com/b2b-events-intelligence-report-2026/)). | Theme: moderate. **Figures: UNVERIFIED** |

### What senior leaders value and hate (observed themes, not a survey)
- **They value** peers at their own level, one sharp question, candour (Chatham House Rule), a small room, and time they control. Organiser copy everywhere now leads with "no sales pitch" (IDC executive dinners, IOActive, CEC Managers+), which tells you what guests have learned to fear.
- **They hate** pitches dressed up as dinners, rooms padded with the wrong job titles, presentation-heavy formats ("watching TV", Forrester), irrelevant outreach, and webinars that turn out to be demos. Educational webinars show 53% more ROI than product-demo ones (vendor data, [Contrast survey of 524 B2B marketers](https://www.getcontrast.io/learn/webinar-statistics)).
- **Inferred:** almost all published content is written for the host or sponsor. The guest's own voice ("why I said yes, why I walked out") is close to absent online. That's a gap (see section 3).

## 3. Competitor content: who owns these topics

### Who's visible (observed in the 10 Nov search spot-check)
| Query (Firecrawl, UK/global) | Who appeared |
|---|---|
| executive roundtable sponsorship UK | executiveroundtables.co.uk (VF Media), Housing Digital, Inonda, Solace, ELN, IRM UK, Arena, MyOutreach |
| host an executive dinner for prospects London | Chill Out (agency), MarTech Summit dinners, Converve, ELN, then restaurants and private-dining sites |
| virtual roundtable executives B2B guide | ELN, ConvergeX, Ortus Club, Evntiqa, BeaconLive, Hoppier, Markletic, MyOutreach |
| executive dinner B2B pipeline guide | Vendelux, BoothIQ, Converve, Engineerica |
| B2B webinar best practices 2026 | ON24, MyOutreach, Kaltura, Univid, EasyWebinar, WebinarNinja |

### Owners by type
- **Event-tech platforms (own the "how to" and stats content):** Bizzabo, Cvent (which now owns ON24), Vendelux, Goldcast, Contrast, Livestorm, Univid, Converve, BoothIQ, Swapcard, vFairs.
- **Research and media (own the authority):** Forrester, Gartner, Amex GBT, Edelman-LinkedIn, Demand Gen Report, and in the UK, MIT (Meetings & Incentive Travel) magazine and B2B Marketing (which runs its own peer-led roundtable forum).
- **Direct curated organisers (closest to us):** ELN / Executive Leaders Network (UK, very similar positioning: ICP-matched guests, "you approve every one"), ConvergeX Connections, Be Executive Events, MyOutreach, Ortus Club, Global Leaders, CM-Alliance, Executive Roundtables (VF Media), Inonda, Mill-All.
- **Publisher and analyst-run dinners:** IDC executive dinners, MarTech Summit, IRM UK.
- **Customer alternative:** vendors running their own dinners (for example, Demandbase's invitation-only London dinner).

**Fair note (observed):** guest-list sign-off by the host isn't unique to us. ELN states it publicly, and MyOutreach and others promise "guaranteed attendance". Our wedge needs to rest on what we won't do (no promised leads or ROI, no pitching, no padding with the wrong titles, no rushed timelines), not on sign-off alone.

### Overused angles (inferred from the set above)
1. Dinner versus trade-show booth cost comparisons.
2. "Turn your dinner into pipeline" and lead-capture framing.
3. "No slides, no pitch" as a bare claim, which almost every organiser now says.
4. Stat round-ups (60, 390, 40 stats).
5. Webinar attendance benchmarks and the best time or day to host.
6. "Guaranteed attendance / guaranteed ROI" promises.
7. AI personalisation and matchmaking inside events.
8. 8-15 guest sizing advice, repeated in every guide.

### Empty angles (gaps we can own)
1. **The guest's side.** Why a Director gives up an evening, and what makes them walk out. Almost everything is written for hosts.
2. **Host behaviour in the room.** What a host does at minute 40 when tempted to pitch, and how a room is lost. Nobody covers the host's own discipline.
3. **The honest timeline.** 12 weeks minimum (2 prep, 10 recruitment). No competitor publishes a recruitment timeline, and the no-show content treats symptoms.
4. **What "Director+" really means.** Title inflation, and why a full room of the wrong titles is a failure. Competitors celebrate headcount.
5. **Failure stories and honest measurement.** What a sponsor should and shouldn't expect, without promised leads. It answers the 38%-of-CMOs gap from the guest-value side.
6. **The table question.** How to write the 1 question a room of peers will argue about. Converve touches it in one line, and no one owns it.
7. **UK-first content.** Nearly all ranking guides are US-centric (dollars, US cities). UK English, pounds and London rooms are an open lane.
8. **Small senior webinars (25-50 Senior Manager+).** Every webinar piece chases volume.
9. **The networking-effectiveness collapse (46% to 15%) explained as a curation problem**, not a software problem.
