About the partner.

Who they are, what they do and why they sit at the table.

Convene X Events designs and delivers invitation-only environments where senior leaders can speak openly: private executive dinners in London, closed virtual roundtables and professionally produced webinars for audiences in the UK, Europe and the US. Every room is built around one question that matters, with guests curated for seniority, sector and a direct stake in the conversation.

The company is founder-led. Before establishing Convene X Events, our founder spent years designing and delivering senior-level events and executive programmes for global enterprise organisations, achieving consistent results throughout his career.

Nothing is sold at the table. There are no panels, no pitches and no audience. Guests come for the quality of the other people in the room and the candour the format allows, and partners come for the same reason.

What they bring to the room.

Every partner profile sets out what the organisation contributes, so guests know why they are at the table.

Curated rooms

Guest lists built person by person for seniority, relevance and fit with the question, never for volume.

Venues chosen for conversation

Private dining rooms and leadership venues across Mayfair, the West End and the City, selected for privacy, acoustics and comfort.

Chairing and moderation

Every room is hosted by an expert moderator, who keeps the conversation balanced, candid and useful to everyone present.

Continuity afterwards

Personal introductions after each room and a private delegate group where the conversation continues at the same senior level.
