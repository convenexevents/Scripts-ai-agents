Covent Garden Hotel, part of Firmdale, has two private rooms on its lower ground floor, both named after nearby West End theatres, and they can be used apart or together.

**The Lyric Room** is intimate and richly decorated. It seats 20 for private dining, 14 around a boardroom table, and holds 30 for a reception. For a confidential dinner of 12 to 16, it's the right size.

**The Fortune Room** is more relaxed and pattern-laden. It seats 32 for private dining, 24 around a boardroom table, and holds 60 for a reception.

Opened together, **the Fortune and Lyric Rooms** seat 48 for private dining or 70 for a reception. That's useful for a two-part evening: drinks in one room while the table is finished in the other, or a larger sector dinner at several tables.

The hotel also has **Tiffany's Library**, a small space for up to 6, and a **Screening Room** for 40, which can host a short briefing or film before dinner.

This is a good fit for marketing, creative and technology leaders who'd rather spend an evening in Seven Dials than a formal Mayfair dining room. The hotel is at 10 Monmouth Street, WC2H 9HB.
