## 2. New venue-specific copy block (230 words)

Most of The Dorchester's event rooms are built for larger occasions, so for an executive dinner the shortlist is short.

**The Penthouse and Pavilion**, for 16 to 64, opens onto a private roof garden with views across the city skyline. For a senior dinner of 16 to 24 with a drinks welcome outside in good weather, it's the strongest choice for that size.

**The Park Suite**, for 10 to 60, can be used whole or as its two halves, which helps when numbers are still moving. **The Holford Room**, for 26 to 50, has a distinctly British style and suits a fuller dinner.

**The Business Suite**, spanning the Boardroom and the Study in a discreet location, is built for focused meetings, and could anchor a working session before dinner elsewhere in the hotel.

**The Chef's Table**, within the kitchens of The Grill, seats up to 12. It's lively by design, with the energy of the kitchen around you, so it suits a relaxed peer evening better than a confidential discussion.

The larger rooms, the Orchid Room (42 to 171), the Gold and Silver Room (40 to 250, with its own Park Lane entrance) and the ballroom, are for receptions and gala-scale events. For a table of 12 to 25 senior leaders, start with the Penthouse or the Park Suite. The hotel is at 53 Park Lane, W1K 1QA.
