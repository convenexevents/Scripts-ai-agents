The Beaumont's private rooms seat from 10 to 45, and each can be set around one boardroom table, which suits a dinner built around a single conversation.

**The Sargent Room**, on the ground floor, has natural daylight, mahogany panelling and paintings by Kees van Dongen and John Singer Sargent. Its hand-made boardroom table seats 20, or it can be laid for up to 30 at three tables. A screen is built in for presentations.

**The Lotos Room**, at 68 m², is panelled in banded cherrywood and walnut and hung with portraits of 1920s and 1930s film stars by Hollywood photographer George Hurrell. An 18-seat boardroom table sits at its centre; set for dining, it takes 45 at separate tables, or 60 for drinks.

**The Munnings Room**, at 72 m², seats up to 40 at four tables or 60 standing, with a 24-seat boardroom table available for business use.

For a peer dinner of 16 to 20, the Sargent Room's single table or the Lotos Room's 18-seat table puts everyone in one conversation, which is how we'd run it. The Munnings Room suits a larger dinner of 30 to 40 at separate tables, with a drinks welcome in the same room. The hotel is at 8 Balderton Street, W1K 6TF.
