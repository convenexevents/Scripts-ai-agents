The Soho Hotel suits a dinner that wants character as well as privacy, with art on the walls and rooms that adapt to the guest list.

**The Sandra Blow Room** is named for the English painter whose contemporary work hangs there. At 43 m², it seats 24 for private dining or around a boardroom table, and holds 50 for a reception. A single table of 16 to 24 fits comfortably, which is the shape we'd use for a peer dinner built around one question.

**The Indigo Room**, at 72 m², was decorated by Kit Kemp and seats up to 50 for private dining, 24 around a boardroom table, or 100 for a reception. It suits a larger dinner at several tables, or a reception before a smaller dinner in the Sandra Blow Room.

Both rooms can be joined with the hotel's other private spaces for larger events. The hotel also has The Crimson Bar and its own screening rooms, which open up options for a short screening or briefing before dinner.

The setting works especially well for marketing, media and technology leaders, who tend to respond to a room with personality more than to a formal boardroom. The hotel is at 4 Richmond Mews, W1D 3DH, in Soho.
