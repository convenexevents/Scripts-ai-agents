Haymarket Hotel, part of Firmdale, gives you a choice between two well-proportioned dining rooms and one dramatic one.

**The Nash Room**, named for the Regency architect, is an elegant, home-from-home room for private dining, meetings or small receptions. It seats 22 for dinner or around a boardroom table, with 30 to 35 for a reception.

**The Rocklin Room**, named for the artist Peter Rocklin, whose work hangs here and across Firmdale's hotels, is described by the hotel as dynamic yet discreet. It also seats 22 for dinner or around a boardroom table.

**The Shooting Gallery** is the headline space: a vast room brought down to a human scale by de Gournay wallpaper of jungle landscapes in grey sepia, furnished with 1970s lucite tables and lamps and pictures by Oliver Messel of his costume designs for Antony and Cleopatra. It seats 40 to 70 for dinner and holds 120 for a reception, with a 5-metre ceiling.

For a single-table executive dinner of 14 to 22, the Nash or Rocklin Room is the right size. The Shooting Gallery suits a larger sector dinner, or a reception for 60 to 120 before a smaller dinner next door. The hotel's swimming pool and bar can also be hired for private events. It's at 1 Suffolk Place, SW1Y 4HX.
