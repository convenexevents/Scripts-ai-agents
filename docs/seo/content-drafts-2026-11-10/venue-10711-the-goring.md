The Goring's five private spaces range from an 8-seat boardroom to a garden room for 44, so it covers most executive formats under one roof in Belgravia.

**The Drawing Room**, at 27 m², hosts up to 12. Silverware, personalised menus and a hidden screen make it suited to a working dinner where someone may need to share a few slides.

**The Silver Room** has an antique dining table and, the hotel says, a history: Winston Churchill once held meetings here. It suits a boardroom-style meeting or an intimate dinner.

**The Boardroom**, discreetly placed on the fourth floor, takes up to 8 for working lunches.

**The Veranda**, an orangery with floor-to-ceiling windows over The Goring Garden, seats 18 at one long table or 30 across three. It's semi-private, so it works for a relaxed peer lunch more than a confidential dinner.

**The Garden Room** is the largest private room, at 86 m², with a sliding glass wall onto the private garden. It seats 44 for dinner or 20 around a boardroom table.

For a board-level dinner of 10 to 12, the Drawing Room or the Silver Room keeps the conversation close and private. For a cross-sector table of 20, the Garden Room in boardroom layout gives everyone a seat at one table. The hotel says its Dining Room holds a Michelin star, which helps when the menu matters to the guest list.
