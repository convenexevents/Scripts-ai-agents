Claridge's has several small private rooms, which makes it easy to match the room to the guest list rather than the other way round.

For a board-level dinner of 10 to 16, **the Boardroom** on the mezzanine has floor-to-ceiling windows, natural daylight and its own private reception area, so guests can arrive and talk before sitting down without crossing a public space. It takes 5 to 16.

**The Dante Mayfair Private Dining Room** seats up to 16, tucked behind double doors off the restaurant, with curated artwork and warm interiors. **The Snuggery**, adjoining Claridge's Bar, takes up to 14, seated or standing.

For a fuller dinner, **the St James Room** on the mezzanine offers private dining for up to 20, with daylight and views over Brook Street, and connects to the Clarence Room when numbers grow. **The Foyer Private Dining Room** seats up to 24 and is available from breakfast through to dinner. **The Highgrove Room**, with Art Deco design, takes 16 to 35.

The Boardroom suits a confidential dinner for CFOs or general counsel. The St James Room or the Foyer suits a cross-sector dinner of 18 to 24. The hotel is on Brook Street, W1K 4HR, in Mayfair.
